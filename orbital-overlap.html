Why?

Positive vs negative vs zero

Positive overlap: overlapping regions have the same phase overall.
Negative overlap: overlapping regions have opposite phase overall.
Zero overlap: positive and negative contributions cancel exactly by symmetry.

How to use + / −

Choose the sign of the lobes entering the overlap region. Thus + with + and − with − give constructive (positive) overlap when the orbital orientations are compatible. + with − or − with + give destructive (negative) overlap. If the orbitals are orthogonal by symmetry, the overlap remains zero regardless of these signs.
